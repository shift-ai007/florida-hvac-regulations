# Florida HVAC Regulations Guide

A comprehensive reference for Florida HVAC licensing, permit requirements, building codes, and regulations. Written for homeowners hiring contractors and professionals navigating Florida's licensing system.

Florida's hot, humid climate means HVAC systems run year-round, making proper installation and maintenance critical. This guide covers the regulatory framework that governs HVAC work in the state.

## Why This Matters

Hiring an unlicensed HVAC contractor in Florida can result in:
- Failed inspections and costly rework
- Voided manufacturer warranties
- Insurance claim denials
- Safety hazards from improper installation
- Fines up to $10,000 for the contractor (and liability exposure for the homeowner)

## Florida Climate Zones

Florida spans climate zones 1 and 2 under IECC, with specific efficiency requirements:

- **Zone 1** (South Florida - Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, Monroe): Highest cooling loads, strictest efficiency standards
- **Zone 2** (Central/North Florida): Slightly lower cooling requirements but still demanding
