# HVAC Contractor Scams & Red Flags -- Florida Homeowner's Guide

HVAC scams spike during South Florida's summer months. This guide covers the most common schemes and how to spot them.

## The "Free Inspection" Trap

A company advertises a free or $29 AC inspection. The technician finds an "emergency" -- failing compressor, refrigerant leak, cracked coil -- quoting $2,000-$8,000 for immediate repair.

**Red flags:** The quoted repair is "only good today" (false urgency). They can't show you visible evidence. No second opinion is offered.

**What to do:** Get a second opinion from a contractor who charges a diagnostic fee ($75-$150). Never authorize work over $500 without a second quote.

## The Bait-and-Switch

The technician fixes one real minor issue, then claims a second expensive problem that doesn't exist.

**Common fake problems:**
- "Your evaporator coil is leaking" -- ask to see the specific leak location
- "Your compressor is drawing high amps" -- ask for measured amperage vs nameplate
- "Your ductwork has toxic mold" -- requires lab testing, not visual
- "Your refrigerant is low" -- a legitimate tech must find and fix the leak (EPA requirement)

## The License and Insurance Lie

An unlicensed operator uses a legitimate contractor's number or claims a handyman exemption.

**Red flags:** Can't produce a physical license. Name on license doesn't match company name. Asks for cash only. Magnetic sign on van instead of painted logo.

**Verify at:** https://www.myfloridalicense.com/wl11.asp

## The "No Permit Needed" Dodge

The contractor says a permit isn't needed for your AC replacement. In Florida, AC replacement ALWAYS requires a permit ($75-$350 depending on county).

**Risk:** Unpermitted work voids insurance and manufacturer warranty, and must be disclosed when selling.

## Payment Scams

- **Cash only / no contract**: Florida law requires written contract for work over $1,000
- **100% upfront**: Maximum legal deposit is 10% or $2,500 (whichever is less)
- **Financing markup**: Compare cash price to financed price -- they should match

## 5-Step Verification Before Hiring

1. **Verify license**: myfloridalicense.com -- CAC or CMC must be active
2. **Check insurance**: Ask for certificate, call agent to verify
3. **Get 3 quotes**: Never hire the first company
4. **Read reviews**: Look for patterns, not isolated complaints
5. **Get it in writing**: Scope, equipment, price, warranty, payment schedule
