# HVAC Permit Fees by South Florida County (2026)

Permit fees vary significantly across Florida counties. This guide covers the major South Florida counties where most residential HVAC work occurs.

## Miami-Dade County

Miami-Dade uses a tiered fee schedule based on project value:

| Project Value | Permit Fee |
|--------------|------------|
| Up to $1,000 | $75 minimum |
| $1,001–$5,000 | $75 + $8 per $1,000 over $1,000 |
| $5,001–$25,000 | $107 + $6 per $1,000 over $5,000 |
| $25,001–$100,000 | $227 + $5 per $1,000 over $25,000 |

**Additional fees**: Plan review (65% of permit fee), technology surcharge ($4), training surcharge ($2).

**Where to apply**: [Miami-Dade Building Department](https://www.miamidade.gov/permits/)

**Typical AC replacement permit**: $150–250 (system + plan review + surcharges)

## Broward County

Broward County permit fees are set by individual municipalities, but most follow this range:

| HVAC Work Type | Typical Fee Range |
|----------------|-------------------|
| AC changeout (like-for-like) | $100–175 |
| New AC installation | $175–300 |
| Ductwork modification | $100–200 |
| Full system + ductwork | $250–400 |

**Plan review**: Required for all new installations. Not required for like-for-like changeouts in most Broward cities.

**Where to apply**: Through the municipality where the property is located. Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Hollywood, and Coral Springs each have their own building departments.

## Palm Beach County

Palm Beach County uses a value-based formula similar to Miami-Dade:

| Project Value | Permit Fee |
|--------------|------------|
| Up to $500 | $50 minimum |
| $501–$2,000 | $50 + $10 per $500 over $500 |
| $2,001–$25,000 | $80 + $7.50 per $1,000 over $2,000 |
| Over $25,000 | $252.50 + $5 per $1,000 over $25,000 |

**Where to apply**: [Palm Beach County Building Division](https://discover.pbcgov.org/pzb/building/)

## How to Estimate Your Permit Cost

1. **Get a written quote** from your contractor (total installed price)
2. **Look up your county's fee schedule** (links above or your city's building department)
3. **Add plan review fees** (usually 50-65% of the permit fee)
4. **Add surcharges** (technology, training, DBPR — usually $5-15 total)

### Rule of Thumb

For a standard residential AC replacement in South Florida, expect to pay **$100–300 in total permit fees**. This is separate from the contractor's costs and is usually included in their quote.

## Who Pulls the Permit?

**Always the licensed contractor.** In Florida, the licensed contractor (CAC or CMC license holder) is responsible for pulling permits. A homeowner cannot pull a mechanical permit for HVAC work — it requires a licensed contractor.

**Red flag**: If a contractor asks YOU to pull the permit, or says you don't need one for a full system replacement, find a different contractor. This is a sign of an unlicensed operator.

## Online Permit Portals

Most South Florida counties now offer online permitting:

- **Miami-Dade**: eBuild portal
- **Broward**: Varies by city — Fort Lauderdale uses EnerGov, Pembroke Pines uses their own portal
- **Palm Beach**: PZAB Portal

Online permits for simple changeouts (like-for-like replacement, same location, same capacity) can be approved in 1-3 business days. New installations requiring plan review take 5-15 business days.

## Inspection After Installation

Every permitted HVAC installation requires a final inspection. The inspector verifies:

1. Equipment matches permit application (correct model, SEER rating, tonnage)
2. Proper clearances maintained
3. Electrical connections to code (proper disconnect, correct wire gauge)
4. Refrigerant lines properly insulated
5. Condensate drain properly routed (Florida requires secondary drain or float switch)
6. Ductwork meets R-value requirements for location (R-8 minimum in unconditioned spaces)
7. System charges match manufacturer specifications

**Scheduling**: Most counties allow online scheduling 24-48 hours in advance. Your contractor should handle this.

**Failed inspection**: If the inspection fails, the contractor must fix the issues and schedule a re-inspection (usually no additional fee for the first re-inspection).
