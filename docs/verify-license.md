# How to Verify a Florida HVAC Contractor's License

Before hiring any HVAC contractor in Florida, verify their license through official state databases. This takes under 5 minutes and can save you thousands.

## Step-by-Step Verification

### Method 1: Online Lookup (Recommended)

1. Visit [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com/wl11.asp)
2. Select "Construction" as the license type
3. Enter the contractor's name OR license number
4. Review the results for:
   - **License status**: Must show "Current, Active"
   - **License type**: CAC (residential/light commercial) or CMC (unlimited)
   - **Expiration date**: Must be in the future
   - **Disciplinary actions**: Check for any complaints or violations

### Method 2: Phone Verification

Call DBPR directly: **(850) 487-1395**
- Provide the contractor's name or license number
- Ask for current license status and any complaint history

### Method 3: County Building Department

Your local building department maintains records of:
- Active contractors who have pulled permits in the county
- Complaint history specific to your area
- Business tax receipt (occupational license) status

## What to Check

| Field | What You Want to See |
|-------|---------------------|
| License Status | "Current, Active" |
| License Type | CAC or CMC (not expired RA) |
| Expiration Date | Future date |
| Disciplinary Actions | None, or resolved with no ongoing violations |
| Insurance | Current liability + workers comp |
| Qualifier | Name of the qualifying agent matches the company |

## Red Flags

**Walk away immediately if:**

- Contractor cannot or will not provide a license number
- License shows "Null and Void," "Delinquent," or "Suspended"
- License number belongs to a different person or company
- Contractor says they are "licensed in another state" (Florida does not have HVAC license reciprocity)
- They offer a discount for skipping the permit
- They want full payment upfront before starting work

## Insurance Verification

Beyond the license, verify insurance:

1. Ask for a Certificate of Insurance (COI)
2. Call the insurance company to confirm the policy is active
3. Minimum required in Florida:
   - General liability: $300,000
   - Workers compensation: Required if they have any employees

## Filing a Complaint

If you discover a contractor is unlicensed or performing substandard work:

1. **DBPR Complaint Portal**: [File online](https://www.myfloridalicense.com/intentions2.asp?chession=FLdbprGOC)
2. **Phone**: (850) 487-1395
3. **Local building department**: Report unpermitted work directly

Unlicensed contracting in Florida is a criminal offense:
- First offense: First-degree misdemeanor (up to 1 year jail, $1,000 fine)
- Repeat offense: Third-degree felony (up to 5 years, $5,000 fine)
- Penalties increase if the work causes property damage or injury
