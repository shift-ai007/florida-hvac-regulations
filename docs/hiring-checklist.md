# Questions to Ask Before Hiring an HVAC Contractor in Florida

Use this checklist when evaluating HVAC contractors. A reputable contractor will answer all of these without hesitation.

## Before Getting Quotes

### License & Insurance

- [ ] **"What is your Florida HVAC license number?"**
  - Must be a CAC or CMC number
  - Verify at [MyFloridaLicense.com](https://www.myfloridalicense.com/wl11.asp)
  - Don't accept "I'm licensed in another state" - Florida has no reciprocity

- [ ] **"Can I see your Certificate of Insurance?"**
  - General liability: $300,000 minimum
  - Workers compensation: Required if they have employees
  - Call the insurance company to verify the policy is current

- [ ] **"Who will pull the permit?"**
  - Answer should always be: "We will"
  - If they say permits aren't needed for a system replacement - walk away

- [ ] **"How long have you been in business?"**
  - Check the DBPR record for license issue date
  - Look for online reviews spanning multiple years

### Technical Competency

- [ ] **"Will you perform a Manual J load calculation?"**
  - Required for proper sizing in Florida
  - If they size by "rule of thumb" (1 ton per 400-500 sq ft) - red flag
  - Ask to see the calculation before signing

- [ ] **"What SEER2 rating do you recommend for my home?"**
  - Minimum 15 SEER2 required in South Florida
  - Should discuss efficiency vs. cost tradeoff for your specific situation
  - Higher SEER = more savings in Florida's 10-month cooling season

- [ ] **"What brand and model will you install?"**
  - Should be specific - not "we'll figure it out"
  - Research the model's reliability and warranty
  - Verify it meets Florida's minimum efficiency requirements

- [ ] **"What does your proposal include?"**
  - Line-item breakdown preferred
  - Must include: equipment, labor, permits, disposal of old system
  - Ask about: thermostat, pad, disconnect, line set, drain line

### The Proposal

- [ ] **"Is this a written contract?"**
  - Required by Florida law for work over $2,500
  - Must include: license number, total price, timeline, scope of work
  - Read the fine print on warranties and payment terms

- [ ] **"What is your warranty on labor?"**
  - Good contractors: 1-2 years minimum
  - Ask what's covered: refrigerant charge, duct connections, electrical, drainage

- [ ] **"Will you register the equipment warranty?"**
  - Usually must be done within 60-90 days of install
  - Extended warranty (5-12 years) requires registration
  - Ask who handles warranty claims after the installation

- [ ] **"What's your payment schedule?"**
  - Reasonable: deposit (10-30%), then balance at completion
  - Red flag: Full payment before work starts
  - Red flag: Cash only / no receipts

## During Installation

- [ ] Verify the permit is posted at the job site
- [ ] Confirm the equipment delivered matches the contract
- [ ] Ask about the expected timeline and inspection schedule
- [ ] Note any changes to the original scope (get change orders in writing)

## After Installation

- [ ] **Inspect the work before making final payment**
  - Equipment secured and level on pad
  - Ductwork properly connected and sealed
  - Condensate drain running to approved location
  - Thermostat programmed and functioning
  - System cycles properly (cool air at all vents)

- [ ] **Verify the inspection passed**
  - Check your county's online permit portal
  - The permit should show "Final" or "Passed" status
  - If it shows "Open" weeks after completion - follow up

- [ ] **Get all documentation**
  - Warranty registration confirmation
  - Equipment manual and filter specifications
  - Maintenance schedule recommendations
  - Final invoice and lien release

## Scoring Guide

| Criteria | Green Flag | Yellow Flag | Red Flag |
|----------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| License | Active CAC/CMC, easily verified | Claims to be "getting" license | No license or wrong type |
| Insurance | COI provided, verified | "I'll send it later" | No insurance or expired |
| Permit | "We always pull permits" | Vague about permits | "You don't need a permit" |
| Load calc | Manual J before sizing | "I'll size it based on your house" | "1 ton per 500 sq ft" |
| Written proposal | Detailed line items | Verbal quote only | Pressure to sign immediately |
| Payment | Milestone-based schedule | Full payment before start | Cash only, no receipts |
| Reviews | Consistent 4+ stars, specific praise | Few reviews | No reviews or many complaints |
